Browser connection setup
  1. Keep the pm executable at a stable location. Choose your browser below, copy the completed registration command, and run it once in a terminal. Your extension ID is already included.
  2. Run pm start. For your first vault, open the desktop app with pm and choose Create your first vault.
  3. Select Check connection again below. If the browser still cannot find the host, close and reopen the browser, then check again.
